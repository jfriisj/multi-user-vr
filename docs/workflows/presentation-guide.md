# Presentation Guide (Run‑of‑Show · Professional)

A structured plan to deliver a polished demo and handle questions.

## Run‑of‑Show (10–15 minutes)
1) Intro (1 min): Problem, vision, and use cases (training, education, team building)
2) Tech Overview (1 min): Unity 2022.3, Meta Quest 3, XRI, NGO, safety‑first design
3) Live Demo (6–10 min): Scenarios 1–4 in order; keep momentum
4) Metrics Snapshot (1 min): Show FPS/RTT briefly; confirm targets met
5) Q&A (2–3 min): Take targeted questions; offer deeper dives post‑demo

## Slide/Display Tips
- Use a single slide with architecture overview and performance targets
- Optional device casting to a display; turn off if it adds lag or instability

## Speaking Tips
- Short, outcome‑focused statements; avoid jargon
- Narrate what viewers should notice (alignment, smooth sync, safety escalation)
- Acknowledge limitations honestly; highlight roadmap items briefly

## Risk Management
- Pre‑run all scenarios; have fallback A/B/C (see Demo Setup)
- Keep debug UI off unless needed; avoid distracting overlays
- If tracking degrades: pause, re‑light, recalibrate guardian, resume

## Timekeeping
- Tech Operator signals time checkpoints; skip to next scenario if behind
- Aim to finish early; leave buffer for Q&A

## Follow‑Up
- Share build notes and performance metrics captured during demo
- Offer next steps for pilot or research collaboration
