What’s your hypothesis?.
Hypothesis
We believe that [customer segment] has a problem with [x] when [situation]
Example: We believe that first-time parents struggle to find trustworthy childcare
Measure
We will know this is true when, in interviews, [X out of Y people] confirm [specific pain]
Example: .. When at least 7 out of 10 parents mention trust as their top concern

Why do user research?
User research helps you:
→ Validating the assumed need
→ Qualifying your product (in the long run)
→ Create ambassadors

Overall goal with
your user research
That you (and your eventual team) validates
whether the need/problem is real or not…
… by interviewing at least 10 private or
3 business target customers

---

Ground rules for
Interviewing
1. Be curious
2. Adopt a beginner’s mind
3. Listen more than you talk
4. Keep asking “why”
5. The goal is insights, not selling

---

.. and remember! F*CK

• Do interviews to understand the depth of
the problem – and ask follow up question
